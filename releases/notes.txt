Ultrawide screens

- No more black bars on ultrawide (21:9, 32:9) and 16:10 screens: the game now fills your whole screen, with no resolution change needed. The HUD stays in the middle. Nothing changes on a 16:9 screen.
- To get the black bars back, set ultrawide = false in NightreignPvP\nrpvp.toml (an update puts it back to true).

Arena against bots

- Fixed: in a Trio match against bots, round 2 could start with only one enemy bot instead of three.
- Duo and Trio against bots now show the killfeed when a bot dies. Your bot allies show in your colour, the enemy bots in red. Solo against a bot stays as before (the round message says who won).

Everyone in a match needs the same mod version. This player build supports up to 6 players and starts with at least 2 after the fill wait.
